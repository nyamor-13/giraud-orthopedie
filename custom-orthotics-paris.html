Services

What the practice offers

Assessment

Foot & gait assessment

Interview, clinical examination, static analysis on a pressure plate and dynamic analysis of walking and running on a treadmill. You receive a written report.

Feet

Custom foot orthotics

Insoles made to measure for heel pain, flat or high-arched feet, forefoot pain, bunions or sport. Made for your shoes: everyday, running, court, cycling or ski boots.

Hand & wrist

Custom thumb & wrist orthoses

Thermoplastic orthoses moulded on your hand: thumb arthritis, carpal tunnel, tendinitis, after a fracture or sprain.

Toes

Silicone toe orthoses

Moulded on your toes to protect or space them: claw or hammer toes, corns, rubbing.

Night

Bunion night splints

Worn at night to support the position of the big toe and the comfort of the forefoot.

Sport

Sports & ski bootfitting

Running, trail, tennis, padel, team sports, triathlon, skiing. Pierre-Emmanuel is a marathon runner himself.

How it works

From assessment to fitting

1

The assessment

About 45 minutes. Bring the shoes you wear most, including sports shoes, and any prescription or medical reports.

2

Made in the workshop

Your orthotics are made by hand on site, not outsourced, so lead times are short. Tell us if you have a race or a trip coming up.

3

Fitting & follow-up

Orthotics are tried in your shoes and adjusted on the spot. They are guaranteed for six months: adjusted or remade until you are comfortable.
